Ridgeback Hut¶

Ridgeback Hut is a stone shelter on the saddle between Kessel Peak and the Long Ridge, at 1,640 metres. The club rebuilt it in 2019 after a winter storm took off the old tin roof. It sleeps fourteen on two wooden platforms and is open all year.

Booking¶

Bunks are booked through the club office, not at the hut. Members pay 18 a night and non-members 26; children under twelve stay free with an adult. Bookings open on the first Monday of March for the summer season. Groups of more than six need the warden's approval at least three weeks ahead.

Facilities¶

The hut has a wood stove, a gas ring with two burners, pots and enamel mugs, and a rain tank on the north wall. There is no electricity. Bring a sleeping bag; blankets were removed in 2022 because of mice.

Item Where
Spare gas canisters Locked box under the left platform (code from the office)
First-aid kit Shelf by the door
Radio Above the stove, channel 6 to the valley ranger station

Getting there¶

From Kessel village

Follow the red waymarks from the church car park up the Millrace path. The climb takes about four hours; the last hour above the tree line is exposed and the club advises turning back if the wind gauge at the forestry gate reads above 60 km/h.

In winter

Between December and March the Millrace path is closed below the waterfall because of avalanche risk. Use the longer forestry road instead and allow six hours. Crampons are required above the saddle.

Other huts

Try Cold Tarn Hut, two hours north, which has a sauna and sleeps twenty.
